MatriceChat is a Matrix client based on SchildiChat Next and Element X, with additional features.

The Matrix protocol is a decentralized approach to modern messaging, allowing you to select a server provider of your choice (or even self-host your own server), enabling you to communicate with end-to-end encryption, shared message history across multiple devices, and more.

MatriceChat is open source: https://github.com/tass-suderman/matricechat
More information about the Matrix protocol: https://matrix.org/
